## 2. Character-by-Character Technical Specifications

### A. Civilian Starting Avatar
- **Identity**: Unnamed Civilian Explorer visiting the Citadel.
- **Continuity**: Neutral contemporary urban visitor.
- **Silhouette**: Regular athletic build (height: 1.75m), hooded casual windbreaker, dark jeans, lightweight trainers.
- **Animation Direction**:
  - Relaxed idle breathing.
  - Symmetrical walk (2.8 m/s) with natural shoulder and pelvic counter-rotations.
  - Forward-leaning athletic sprint (5.6 m/s).
  - Confident jump lift and cushioned landing.

### B. Miles Morales (Earth-1610)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (Sony Pictures Animation, 2023).
- **Silhouette**:
  - Lean teenage frame (height: 1.73m).
  - Longer, springy limbs; narrow waist and athletic runner shoulders.
  - Distinctly lighter and more lithe than adult Peter B. Parker.
- **Suit & Mask**:
  - Base: Deep matte black compression fabric with subtle crimson micro-ribbing.
  - Emblems: Hand-stenciled red spray-painted spider on chest; red side-stripes running from armpits down the torso flanks to the thighs.
  - Mask: Contoured white lenses with sharp red enclosing perimeter; smooth head profile with no exterior stitching seams.
  - Footwear: Custom red/black high-top athletic footwear.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Springy, buoyant locomotion; relaxed asymmetrical idle.
  - Bio-electric venom energy crackles during ability execution.

### C. Peter B. Parker (Earth-616)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* / *Across the Spider-Verse* mentor appearance.
- **Silhouette**:
  - Mature adult build (height: 1.83m).
  - Broader chest, heavier torso, slightly tired/relaxed shoulder posture.
- **Suit & Mask**:
  - Classic dual-tone crimson chest/shoulders and navy blue limbs.
  - Symmetrical black webbing lattice.
  - Bold white lenses with thick black borders.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Grounded, economical combat veteran stride.
  - Stable foot placement; no excessive bounce.

### D. Gwen Stacy / Spider-Gwen (Earth-65)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse*.
- **Silhouette**:
  - Lean, athletic, acrobatic dancer frame (height: 1.70m).
  - Volumetric fabric hood framing the head with realistic clearance.
- **Suit & Mask**:
  - Upper: Crisp white hood, shoulders, and chest.
  - Interior Hood Lining: Teal/cyan web lattice pattern on neon magenta backdrop.
  - Torso/Legs: Matte black tights with hot pink accent webbing under arms.
  - Footwear: Turquoise ballet climbing slippers.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Agile, poised, rhythmic movement; light step turnover.

### E. Miguel O'Hara / Spider-Man 2099 (Earth-928)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse*.
- **Silhouette**:
  - Imposing, broad-shouldered, muscular V-taper (height: 1.95m, scaled 1.15x relative to normal humanoid).
  - Heavy upper torso and forearm silhouette.
- **Suit & Mask**:
  - Base: Deep iridescent midnight blue/indigo.
  - Emblems: Angular crimson fanged skull emblem spanning across the chest and shoulders.
  - Arm Blades: Retractable red hard-light talons along the outer forearms.
  - Mask: Sharp angular visor slit lenses; aggressive predatory brow line.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Weighty, commanding, deliberate movement with rapid acceleration.

### F. Hobie Brown / Spider-Punk (Earth-138)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse*.
- **Silhouette**:
  - Tall, lanky, angular frame (height: 1.88m).
  - Layered punk battle vest, studded shoulder pads, high lace-up Dr. Martens boots.
- **Costume & Mask**:
  - Mask: Silver chrome punk spikes forming a 6-stud mohawk along the center skull.
  - Vest: Bleached denim jacket with torn collar, anarchist pins, and back spider patch.
  - Back: Custom electric Gibson guitar slung across the back strap.
- **Rendering & Animation**:
  - Halftone print textures; aggressive, rhythmic walking gait.

### G. Pavitr Prabhakar / Spider-Man India (Earth-50101)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* film design.
- **Silhouette**:
  - Lean, agile martial artist build (height: 1.74m).
  - Voluminous wavy black hair flowing uncompressed out from the top mask crown.
- **Costume & Equipment**:
  - Suit: Saffron red and deep blue suit with gold filigree web tracery.
  - Lower Body: Film-accurate blue pleated dhoti wrap (NOT the comic white dhoti).
  - Wrists: Polished gold bangles acting as spinning web guides.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Kalaripayattu martial footwork; fluid, buoyant, celebratory locomotion.

### H. Peni Parker & SP//dr (Earth-14512)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (SP//dr spherical mech).
- **Physical Representation**:
  - The playable avatar in 3D exploration is the **SP//dr Biomechanical Armor Mech** (height: 2.3m, scale 1.25x).
  - Chassis: Glossy red and off-white spherical titanium armor.
  - Visor: Emotive cyan LED digital display showing real-time facial emoticons.
  - Limbs: Reinforced cybernetic joints with thruster exhaust nozzles on the back.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Heavy servo-assisted bipedal strides; mechanical shock-absorption on jumps.
  - Separate camera distance offset (6.2m) to frame the mech volume properly.

### I. Spider-Man Noir (Earth-90214)
- **Continuity Target**: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*.
- **Silhouette**:
  - 1933 Great Depression detective silhouette (height: 1.85m).
  - Broad-brimmed felt fedora hat; heavy dark wool trench coat with upturned collar.
- **Costume & Materials**:
  - Mask: Stitched dark leather cowl with brass-rimmed pilot aviator goggles.
  - Values: High-contrast charcoal, graphite, and jet black separation to prevent flat visual clipping.
- **Animation Language**:
  - Deliberate, hardboiled, measured pacing; hands poised near coat pockets.
