# Spider-Verse Character Research & Asset Attribution Dossier

This document establishes the official research, continuity choices, lore specifications, and 3D asset attributions for the **Spider-Verse Explorer** multiversal experience.

---

## 1. Chosen Continuity Framework

All 8 characters are grounded primarily in the **Sony Pictures Animation *Spider-Verse* cinematic continuity** (*Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* [2018] and *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* [2023]), with canonical Marvel Comic references cited for initial publication history.

We strictly distinguish variants (e.g., Peter B. Parker Earth-616 vs. Peter Parker Noir Earth-90214 vs. Miles Morales Earth-1610) to avoid conflating comic-mainstream, live-action MCU, or video game continuities.

---

## 2. Character Lore & Verified Specifications

### 1. Miles Morales (Spider-Man)
- **Continuity**: Earth-1610 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Miles Gonzalo Morales.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-1610 (Brooklyn, New York).
- **Origin & Bio**: Bitten by a radioactive Alchemax spider (Alchemax Earth-42 specimen transported to Earth-1610). Stepped up following the death of his world's Peter Parker. Crafted his own identity with spray paint, street art sensibility, and hip-hop musical timing.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Superhuman agility, wall-crawling, precognitive Spider-Sense, **Bio-Electric Venom Blast** (controlled electric discharge), **Active Camouflage** (invisibility), custom high-top sneakers, web-shooters.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Matte black suit with hand-stenciled spray-painted red spider emblem, loose web etchings, red eye contours, and vibrant yellow/cyan bio-electric voltage crackles.
- **Visual Style**: Rhythmic stutter-step animation (12 fps initially, 24 fps once confident), pop-art Ben-Day dots, expressive halftone overlays.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Ultimate Comics: Fallout* #4 (August 2011), created by Brian Michael Bendis and Sara Pichelli.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018), *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 2. Peter B. Parker (Spider-Man)
- **Continuity**: Earth-616 (Sony Spider-Verse mentor variant).
- **Full Identity**: Peter Benjamin Parker.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-616.
- **Origin & Bio**: A 38-year-old veteran Spider-Man who endured 22 years of tragedy, injuries, a divorce from Mary Jane, and burnout before being sucked into Earth-1610. Re-energized by mentoring Miles, he reconciles with MJ and becomes a proud father to Mayday Parker.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Proportional arachnid strength, veteran combat reflexes, precognitive Spider-Sense, dual mechanical web-shooters, improvisational genius.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Classic dual-tone crimson and deep navy suit with black web lining, expressive white lenses with dark borders. Often wears sweatpants, a pink bathrobe, or a baby carrier harness with Mayday.
- **Visual Style**: Classic Silver-Age comic aesthetics, dynamic action lines, subtle comic sound effect bursts (*THWIP*, *POW*).
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Amazing Fantasy* #15 (August 1962), created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018).

### 3. Gwen Stacy (Spider-Gwen / Ghost-Spider)
- **Continuity**: Earth-65 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Gwendolyne Maxine Stacy.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-65.
- **Origin & Bio**: Bitten by a genetically engineered spider in high school. Her best friend Peter Parker sought powers through lizard serum and tragically died, leading police Captain George Stacy (her father) to hunt Spider-Woman. Plays drums in *The Mary Janes*.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Acrobatics influenced by ballet and drummer percussion rhythm, wall-crawling, Spider-Sense, mechanical web-shooters, multiversal Web-Watch.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Sleek white hooded suit with vibrant turquoise/teal web pattern lining, hot magenta accents, black torso tights, teal ballet-style climbing slippers.
- **Visual Style**: Watercolor expressionism that shifts mood colors (pinks, blues, purples) based on emotional interiority.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Edge of Spider-Verse* #2 (September 2014), created by Jason Latour and Robbi Rodriguez.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018), *Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 4. Miguel O'Hara (Spider-Man 2099)
- **Continuity**: Earth-928 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Miguel O'Hara.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-928 (Nueva York, year 2099).
- **Origin & Bio**: Brilliant head geneticist at Alchemax who attempted to purge an addictive corporate drug from his system, resulting in his DNA being spliced 50% with spider genetics. Founder and commander of the Spider-Society Citadel, dedicated to preventing multiversal canon unraveling.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Retractable venomous fangs, razor-sharp forearm talons, accelerated vision/telepathy, hard-light interactive plasma webbing, day-watch multiversal portal generator. *Note: Has no precognitive Spider-Sense.*
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Imposing physical silhouette, deep indigo-navy suit with striking crimson fanged skull emblem and glowing red hard-light arm blades.
- **Visual Style**: Futuristic neon Cyberpunk, high-contrast digital glitching, geometric hard-light projections, intense scarlet lighting.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Spider-Man 2099* #1 (November 1992), created by Peter David and Rick Leonardi.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 5. Hobie Brown (Spider-Punk)
- **Continuity**: Earth-138 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Hobart "Hobie" Brown.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-138 (Camden, London under Norman Osborn's V.E.N.O.M. regime).
- **Origin & Bio**: Radical punk-rock rebel who liberated his world from authoritarian corporate fascism using 15,000 volts of raw punk rock and electric guitar feedback. Secretly mentors and assists Miles and Gwen.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Wall-crawling, spider-sense, custom electric guitar producing concussive acoustic shockwaves, safety pin fasteners, spiked mask.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Chrome-spiked mohawk mask studs, hand-painted denim battle vest over torn red-and-blue suit with punk patches, bondage straps, Dr. Martens boots.
- **Visual Style**: Hand-drawn xerox zine collage, constantly desynchronized framerate (head, torso, and background rendered at different frame rates and print styles), screen-printed half-tone tears.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *The Amazing Spider-Man* (vol. 3) #10 (January 2015), created by Dan Slott and Olivier Coipel.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 6. Pavitr Prabhakar (Spider-Man India)
- **Continuity**: Earth-50101 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Pavitr Prabhakar.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-50101 (Mumbattan: fusion of Mumbai and Manhattan).
- **Origin & Bio**: Gained mystical arachnid abilities from an ancient yogi. Protects Mumbattan while maintaining an optimistic personality, effortlessly defeating villains, acing his homework, and dating Gayatri Kamath.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Traditional Kalaripayattu martial art combat, spinning web cords guided by enchanted gold bangles, spider-sense, extreme agility.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Vibrant crimson and teal-blue suit with traditional pleated white dhoti trousers, gold embroidered wrist bangles, stylized flowing black hair with zero helmet/mask compression.
- **Visual Style**: Indian Indrajal comic-book typography, ornate gold mandala flourishes, kinetic splash patterns, warm sunset saffron and cyan palette.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Spider-Man: India* #1 (November 2004), created by Jeevan J. Kang, Suresh Seetharaman, and Sharad Devarajan.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 7. Peni Parker & SP//dr
- **Continuity**: Earth-14512 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Peni Parker.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-14512 (Neo-Tokyo).
- **Origin & Bio**: Following the death of her father, Peni allowed herself to be bitten by a radioactive spider that acts as the co-pilot CPU for the SP//dr biomechanical armor. Forms an unbreakable psychic symbiosis with the arachnid core.
- **Powers & Equipment**: SP//dr titanium spherical armored mech, plasma propulsion thrusters, cybernetic web blasters, high-speed tactical computational telemetry, psychic arachnid neural link.
- **Visual & Mask Design**: High-school sailor fuku school uniform alongside the spherical red-and-white SP//dr chassis featuring an emotive multi-state cyan LED eye display.
- **Visual Style**: Retro anime and 90s mecha styling, speed lines, animated manga emoticons, bright pop-digital palette.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Edge of Spider-Verse* #5 (October 2014), created by Gerard Way and Jake Wyatt.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018), *Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).

### 8. Peter Parker (Spider-Man Noir)
- **Continuity**: Earth-90214 (Sony Spider-Verse film continuity).
- **Full Identity**: Peter Parker.
- **Universe Designation**: Earth-90214 (1933 New York).
- **Origin & Bio**: A gritty, idealistic reporter during the Great Depression who investigated an illegal shipment of antiquities and was bitten by an ancient mystical spider from a shattered Spider-God idol. Operates in deep shadows against corrupt syndicates.
- **Powers & Equipment**: Superhuman strength, agility, wall-crawling, spider-sense, organic shadow webbing, marksmanship, hardboiled detective deduction. *Note: Views the entire world exclusively in black, white, and shades of grey.*
- **Visual & Mask Design**: Heavy dark wool trench coat with upturned collar, fedora hat, stitched leather mask with brass-framed pilot aviator goggles.
- **Visual Style**: 1930s film noir cinema, dramatic German expressionist chiaroscuro lighting, venetian blind shadow slashes, rain particles, monochromatic film grain.
- **Official Citations**:
  - Marvel: *Spider-Man Noir* #1 (February 2009), created by David Hine, Fabrice Sapolsky, and Carmine Di Giandomenico.
  - Sony Pictures: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018).

## 3. Asset Attribution & Procedural 3D Architecture

To comply strictly with ethical and licensing guidelines:
1. **SVG Vector Artworks**: Handcrafted, bespoke SVG character portraits designed with accurate costume anatomy, color palettes, and signature geometry for this project.
2. **3D Scene Representation**:
   - The 3D Multiverse Hub is constructed with procedural Three.js / React Three Fiber geometries:
     - Central Go-Home Machine quantum energy platform with layered concentric rings and glowing quantum core.
     - Eight custom pedestals positioned radially at precise 45-degree angles.
     - Holographic portal archways with character-specific chromatic glow.
     - Dimensional floating comic fragments and energy conduits.
   - Character stations render stylized holographic 3D character avatars with glowing dimension shields and particle emissions.
   - The system is architected to allow drop-in replacement with production `.glb` assets via the modular `MULTIVERSE_STATIONS` configuration in `src/data/multiverse-stations.ts`.
